## Hey there

I'm Abhishek Chatterjee, a **Senior Software Engineer I at [SurveyMonkey](https://www.surveymonkey.com/)** with **over 7 years of experience** building scalable backend systems. I primarily specialize in backend engineering, with expertise in **Python**, **Go**, and **JavaScript**, along with a wide range of frameworks and libraries across both backend and frontend development.

At SurveyMonkey, I work on **[LaunchPad](https://www.surveymonkey.com/product/launchpad/)**, the company's flagship market research platform, where I contribute to designing and developing reliable, high-performance backend services.

I regularly work with cloud platforms such as AWS ([AWS Certified](https://www.credly.com/badges/e870c199-cd61-4a2d-8003-fa2c9fe5e47f/linked_in_profile)) and Azure to design and operate scalable backend systems. My experience spans relational and NoSQL databases, distributed services, authentication, caching, and performance optimization.

Beyond my professional work, I'm the founder of [Rocket](https://rocket.imdeepmind.com), an [open-source](https://github.com/imdeepmind/rocket) platform for rapidly building backend APIs; [ayo](https://github.com/imdeepmind/ayo), a self-hosted, end-to-end encrypted, multi-cloud storage platform; and Delphy, a web application for analyzing open-source packages. I've also contributed to the open-source community by creating packages such as [Hocrox](https://github.com/imdeepmind/hocrox), [PCY](https://github.com/imdeepmind/python-completes-you), and [NeuralPy](https://github.com/imdeepmind/NeuralPy).

On this site, I publish my study notes and write blogs, primarily focused on software engineering and programming. You can read the [Introduction](https://imdeepmind.com/blog/imdeepmind-introduction) post to get a clearer idea of what this website is about.

Software engineering is a big part of my life, but it's not the only thing I enjoy.

## Traveling, driving, biking, and hiking

I’ve always loved traveling. Most of my trips are on my bike or in my car, depending on my destination and how much time I have. There’s something about long rides and open roads that clears my mind and makes the journey enjoyable.

Recently, I’ve started hiking, and it’s becoming one of my favorite ways to explore new places. It’s slower and quieter, and it encourages me to soak in the surroundings instead of just passing through. It’s a different kind of travel, but I enjoy it just as much.
